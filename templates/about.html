Showtime

Fashion Marketplace

About

Showtime is a cloud-hosted campus marketplace built for Hampton University students. It lets student brand owners open a storefront, publish clothing listings, and manage inventory, while other students browse, wishlist, and buy directly from them.

How it works

Every student signs up with a Hampton email. From there, anyone can browse storefronts and shop. If you create a storefront of your own, you gain seller tools on top of that same account — there's no separate buyer/seller signup.

What you can do here

  • Browse student-run storefronts and their listings
  • Save items to a wishlist
  • Add items to your bag and check out
  • Create your own storefront and publish listings
  • Request a return on a completed order

Checkout & exchange

Showtime uses an in-person exchange model — no payments are processed through the site. At checkout you provide contact details and a meeting location so the buyer and seller can complete the exchange directly.
